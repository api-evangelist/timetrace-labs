# TimeTrace Labs

> TimeTrace Labs is a London-based mathematics and artificial intelligence company building "the measurement layer for healthcare" — quantitative measurement and analytics that separate signal from noise in multimodal physiological time-series (kinematics, EEG, ECG, actigraphy, glucose, voice). Spun out of a decade of research at the University of Oxford; backed by Seedcamp and Fly Ventures. The company states its outputs power analytics in drug development, public health, and clinical research, accessible through secure APIs and a compliant analytics dashboard, but it publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or machine-readable API specifications yet.
